This policy covers "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state laws. Because this is a sex and love addiction recovery site, we treat the fact that you use it, and anything you log in our trackers, as health information. Our general [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) covers the rest of the site.

SLA Group Resources is run by a volunteer. You can reach us at [slagroupresources@gmail.com](mailto:slagroupresources@gmail.com).

## What we collect, and why

| Health information | Why we collect it | How it's used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you log in the trackers: feelings, how strong they were, notes, 90-day check-ins, urges, coping skills and how much they helped | Only because you start a tracker and ask us to save it, after you agree | To show your entries back to you when you sign in. It's encrypted on your device first, so we can't read it. |
| Reports you choose to share | Only when you create a private link | To show the report to the person you send the link to, until it expires or you remove it. Also encrypted, so we can't read it. |
| The fact that you visited a recovery site: your IP address, browser details, and the pages you opened | Needed to deliver the pages you ask for and keep the site secure | Our hosting and database providers handle it to run the site. Resource pages also send cookie-free page counts that we only see as totals. |
| Anything you choose to write in our suggestion form | Only if you send it | To review suggested resources. Please leave out your own health details. |

Resource ratings are counted as totals and aren't linked to you.

We don't collect precise location, biometric or genetic data, and we don't use geofencing.

## Where it comes from

All of it comes from you, or from your browser when you use the site. We don't buy health data or get it from anyone else.

## Who receives it

We share health information only as needed to run the site, with these service providers. We never sell it, and we never share it for advertising.

| Who | What they receive | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website hosting: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) | IP address, browser details, and the pages you request | [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) |
| Cloud database: Google LLC (Firebase) | Encrypted tracker data and shared reports, plus IP address and browser details when pages use the database | [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |
| Web analytics: Cloudflare, Inc. | Page address, referring site, browser and country for resource pages only, without cookies | [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) |
| Form service: Google LLC (Google Forms) | What you type in the suggestion form | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |

We have no affiliates. When you send a shared report link to someone, you choose who receives it.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your health information
- See it, including the list of everyone we've shared it with
- Withdraw your consent
- Have it deleted

**The fastest way to use these rights is built into the trackers.** "Delete my tracker" erases your saved data and every shared link right away, and you can remove a single shared link any time. Signing in shows you everything we hold for that tracker.

You can also email [slagroupresources@gmail.com](mailto:slagroupresources@gmail.com) with "Privacy request" in the subject. Tell us what you'd like, and we'll:

- Answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you why within those 45 days and finish within another 45.
- Answer for free, up to twice a year.
- Pass any deletion on to the service providers above.

**Never send us your username or passphrase.** We don't know whose tracker is whose, and we can't open any of them, so we can't find tracker data from an email. For tracker data, use "Delete my tracker." For anything else, we may ask a question to make sure the request is really yours.

Withdrawing consent works the same way. Stop using the trackers and delete your tracker, and nothing more will be saved.

We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

## How to appeal

If we turn down your request, you can appeal. Reply to our answer, or email [slagroupresources@gmail.com](mailto:slagroupresources@gmail.com) with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. We'll answer in writing within 45 days and explain our decision.

If we deny your appeal, you can contact the [Washington State Attorney General](https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint).

## Changes

If we start collecting new kinds of health information, or want to use it in a new way, we'll update this page first and ask for your agreement before we do.
