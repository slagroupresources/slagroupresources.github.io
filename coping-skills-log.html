Coping skills log

Write down what happened, how you felt, and what helped. Over time you'll see which coping skills work best for you.

Use this often? Put the coping skills log on your home screen like an app.

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

It shows up as "Coping Log" so it's discreet. Each tracker can have its own icon: add the others from their pages.

Your privacy
  • Your entries are encrypted with your passphrase before they're saved.
  • We can't see your passphrase or anything you log.
  • Someone would need both your username and your passphrase to open your tracker.
  • If you forget your passphrase, we can't get it back. Write it down somewhere safe.
  • Don't use your real name or email as your username.
  • Your password manager can save your username and passphrase for you.

      Situation: Argument with partner
      Thoughts: "I'm a failure in this relationship." "I'm going to end up alone."
      Before: Shame, sadness (9/10)
      Coping skill: Replacement / challenging thoughts: "Just because we argued doesn't mean my partner doesn't care about me."
      Results: Improved mood. Realized I don't have to believe every negative thought.
      After: Shame, sadness (5/10)
