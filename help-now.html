If you're having an urge

  • Make a safety plan. If certain places or people trigger you, plan ahead so you stay safe.
  • Reach out. Call your partner, a friend, or family. You don't have to share details.
  • Check HALT. Are you hungry, angry, lonely, or tired? Take care of that first.
  • Wait it out. Give it 15 minutes and go for a walk.
