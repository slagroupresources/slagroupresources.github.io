Your healing matters on its own.

## You're not alone

S-Anon lists feelings many people have after being hurt by someone else's sexual behavior: feeling betrayed or embarrassed, doubting yourself, and worrying it may be your fault. <sup>[1](#sources)</sup>

## Where to start

- **In crisis?** Call or text [988](tel:988) in the U.S., or call 911.
- **Find support for yourself.** [S-Anon](https://sanon.org/) <sup>[1](#sources)</sup> and [COSA](https://cosa-recovery.org/) <sup>[2](#sources)</sup> are 12-step groups for people affected by someone else's sexual behavior.
- **Ask your partner to [bring the session home](index.html#practices):** take notes in their group and share them with you after.
- **Need a moment?** Go to [Help now](help-now.html).
- **Find resources for you.** On the [homepage](index.html), pick "I was hurt."
